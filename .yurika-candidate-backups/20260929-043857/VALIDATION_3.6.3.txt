Validation summary

Architecture
- MATLAB source defines frequency/resolution numerical shaping and header export contract.
- MATLAB/MATLAB Coder was NOT available on this build host. cpp-ai/generated_numeric_coeffs.h is marked YURIKA_NUMERIC_MATLAB_GENERATED=0 and is a manual mirror of the documented equations.
- C++ performs hierarchical 697-parameter neural inference, causal residual derivative shaping, high-pass synthesis, sanitization and probe metrics.
- C++ is compiled to a local packaged WebAssembly module. WebAssembly.Module imports: 0.
- Dart detector is non-AI and uses typed object indices: 1=micro, 2=local, 3=context. All three retain continuous scores. dominantIndex is diagnostic only.
- JavaScript is browser glue/control and audio block marshalling only.

Important limits
